# Coastal Chaos: Sunset Shores

### A whole city to discover. Plenty of ways to get into trouble.

**[▶ Play now in your browser](https://linuxuniverseofficial.github.io/cc.ss.github.io/)** · no install · keyboard & mouse, controller or touch

Sunset Shores is a 3D open-world sandbox that runs right in your browser. It has neon streets, sunburnt beaches, canals under the bridges and alleys you probably shouldn't walk down at night. Earn a living, cause a scene, chase a rumor or just drive until the sun goes down. The city doesn't care how you spend the day. It only remembers what you did.

And no two cities are the same. **Every map grows from a seed**: new neighborhoods, new shortcuts, new places to find. Share a seed with a friend or roll a fresh one and get lost again.

---

## 🌴 A new beginning

Nathan lands in Sunset Shores from an Atlantic island with money in his pocket, a dream about a convertible and one person on his mind: Claire. She's on the other side of town, on the wrong side of a cell door.

The opening chapter follows **both of them, each on their own side of the city**: a purchase, an escape, a police checkpoint, a phone call. It ends with an ending worth watching at sunset. Then the whole city is yours.

## 🔄 Two lives, one city

Switch between **Nathan and Claire** (Nate and Clary to each other) at any moment. The camera lifts into the sky, crosses the skyline and drops you into the other's life, wherever they are and whatever they're doing. Each has their own wallet, wardrobe and phone. Better still, they can team up and hit the streets together.

## 🚗 What's the plan today?

Start on foot. Grab a car. Head across town.

- **Cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks, speedboats, jet skis, helicopters**, from a humble Kipwell Zip to a Veycross Slipstream R, and a few rides you'll have to find for yourself.
- **Buy your dream car** at the dealership, pick your plate, then make it yours at the workshop: paint, nitro, turbo, neon.
- **Fill up at the gas station**, grab a snack at the convenience store, and try not to get too close to the tire shop's scrapyard next door.
- **Hit a dirt jump out in the countryside**, launch from a back-alley ramp, or **parachute from a rooftop** and aim for the target on the sand.
- **Raise the drawbridges** from the booth, or wait for the boats to pass. Find the hidden **Neon Road**, a street that only lights up for those who know where to look.
- **Grab a skateboard** from your garage and cruise the sidewalks: push, carve, ollie, and eat the pavement when you hit something. Get up to full speed and hold the jump for a **kickflip**, but land it straight or you're kissing the asphalt. Put your headphones on and the radio rides with you. The mall's skate shop sells new decks with custom colors, prints and your own 6-character text.
- **Dress the part**: shirts, prints, caps, glasses, jewelry. Put your own letter, number or symbol on your shirt.

## 💸 Make money. Make trouble.

Pick up taxi fares, take on vigilante work, race at night, or survive a rampage. Spend the cash on clothes, weapons, fuel and upgrades. In free roam, the pause menu's **Minigames** shortcut drops you straight at any casino table, and if you're broke, a loan shark will front you $10,000. Pay it back in ten hours, or his crew comes looking for you, guns out, every few hours until you do.

Try your luck at the **casino**: slots, blackjack, video poker, roulette a **pool table** where Seu Zé is waiting for a game of *castigado* (evens vs odds, and ball 1 is the penalty), and a **Liar's Dice** table where Dona Cida and Tonhão shake their cups the old frontier way: bid, call the bluff, or go for the exact count with *spot on*. Dance until sunrise at the **nightclub**, and maybe have one drink too many: the room starts to spin, and five drinks is the limit. Shop across two floors of the **mall**, and check into the roadside **motel** if you need a place to hide. Some businesses in town have a less respectable side, and the people running them won't hand over the keys without a fight. **Build an empire** if you can hold it.

And there's a bank. With a vault. Just saying.

Don't expect the police to look the other way. Patrol cars, checkpoints, helicopters and local word-of-mouth can turn a quiet afternoon into a five-alarm chase.

## 🌅 A world that lives without you

- **Day turns to night**, the Moon changes phase, and the skyline lights up in neon.
- **Rain forms far away and drifts across the map.** When a storm rolls in, the sky closes, the wind picks up, and the streets change with it.
- **Beaches full of life**: sunbathers, umbrellas, gulls over the shore, a lighthouse at the end of the sand.
- **From the towers of DOWNTOWN to the beachfront of Saltwater Drive**, the quiet streets of Almond Grove, the canals of Cedar Reach, the warehouses of Iron Yard and the dirt roads of Heron Acres, and places that seem to be hiding something. Cross a district line and its name rolls onto the screen, old-school style.

Slow down once in a while. Some discoveries don't come with an announcement.

## 🌀 Beyond the city limits

Sunset Shores isn't the only place you can end up.

- **Parallel worlds**: a synthwave dimension drenched in neon, a dark and silent **Upside Down**, and **Point Haze**, a foggy island of dead trees out at sea, reachable by boat or helicopter.
- **A secret military lab** is buried under Point Haze. Two floors of sealed rooms, a cargo elevator, a containment tank with something floating in it, and Tesla coils you really shouldn't get close to. Break in and see what they're hiding.
- In the Upside Down the doors still open: empty, dark buildings where nothing works, but nothing follows you in either.
- Some masks give more than a disguise. Ever tried running across the water?

## 🌐 Online with your friends

Pick **ONLINE** in the menu and share the city with up to 24 players, SA-MP style: an empty city, just you and your crew.

- **Same city, same sky**: a new map every day, the same hour and the same weather for everyone.
- **Your look, your ride**: choose Nathan or Claire, the clothes, the car and the plate before you join. Export your setup to a file and import it next time.
- **Name and password**: your money, weapons and personal car are waiting when you come back. New accounts start with $500.
- **Chat with T**, see everyone on the map in their shirt color, fight, steal cars from each other (**F**) or **ride along with G** so one drives and the other shoots. Driving a taxi? Your passengers pay the fare.
- **Shared world**: parked cars, weapons and health packs are the same for everyone. Whoever goes down drops their gun and some cash for the fastest hand.
- **Your own safe house**, only you get in. Car blew up? Call **VEHICLE DELIVERY** on the phone or pay at the police station to get it back right now.
- **Local or remote**: play on the site's own server, or type any server address (your PC on the local network, or a remote host). The server is a single PHP file with SQLite; `servidor-online.sh` / `.bat` start one on your machine, and there's a Docker recipe in `online/LEIA-ME.md`.

## 🏠 A place to come home to

Your safe house is where you rest, change clothes, save your progress and **watch TV**. The garage keeps your rides, plates and all, and if you go down out there, your cars and your skateboard are waiting back home when you wake up. The local channels follow what's really happening out there: police operations, storms, chases, departures from the airport, and the occasional strange report from the woods.

## 🕵️ Secrets in every corner

There are records to collect, stunts to land, and dangerous opponents waiting in places you wouldn't expect. Some secrets take patience. Others take nerve.

And some might leave you wondering whether you're still in the same city at all. 👀

## 🎮 See the city your way

- **First person, third person, top-down or isometric**: switch any time.
- **Map + GPS** with route guidance that respects one-way streets, and a marker on your last vehicle so you can always find it again. Inside a building, the map switches to its floor plan.
- **Photo mode**: change the hour, the weather and the lens, add an eclipse or a VHS look, freeze your character and walk the camera around for the perfect angle, or hide them and take the shot.
- **Original soundtrack** for the tense moments, plus radio stations on the road.
- **At night, look up**: once in a while a shooting star crosses the sky.
- **Keyboard & mouse, controllers and touch**, with full mobile support in landscape.
- **DualSense extras** where supported: adaptive triggers, light bar that reacts to your health or a police chase, phone notifications through the controller, and optional **tilt steering** (controller or phone).
- **Graphics presets** to balance looks and performance on any device.

## 🛣️ Before you hit the road

| | |
|---|---|
| **Move** | WASD / arrow keys |
| **Enter / leave vehicles** | F |
| **Look around** | Mouse |
| **Camera** | V |
| **Map** | M |
| **Weapon wheel** | hold TAB, click to attack |
| **Controller / touch** | follow the on-screen prompts · see **CONTROLS** in the menu |

**Saving:** choose **SAVE GAME** in the menu and keep the save file. Use **LOAD** to pick up where you left off. Progress isn't saved automatically.

**Cheats** are part of the fun: bend the rules, unleash wild effects, turn the city into your playground. Using cheats disables saving and activity rewards for that session.

## 🛠️ About the project

Coastal Chaos: Sunset Shores is in active development. It mixes pixel-art characters, simple 3D worlds and city lights with a love for classic open-world games. The campaign is just getting started; free roam already lets the city speak for itself.

It's built as a single web page (three.js) and can be installed as an app (PWA).

Developed through iteration with assistance from Claude and ChatGPT/Codex. Inspirations include classic GTA games, [24/7 CLT by Uniday Studio](https://uniday.studio/games/247-clt-early-access?cur=brl&lang=pt-BR) and [Pat Kerr's vehicle prototype](https://patkerr.co.uk/2d-vehicles/). José's contribution to the police emergency-light controls is also part of the project's history.
